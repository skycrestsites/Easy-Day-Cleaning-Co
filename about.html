About Easy Day Cleaning Co

A Locally Owned Team Built on Trust

We are a professional cleaning company serving homeowners, families, and businesses across Oakland County. Our promise is simple: consistent, reliable cleaning delivered with exceptional care.

Our Story

Built Around Reliability and Care

Founded by Christopher Kayj, Easy Day Cleaning Co was started with a clear purpose: to bring dependable, detail-oriented cleaning to homes and businesses across Oakland County. We saw a need for a cleaning team that homeowners and professionals could truly count on, with the kind of communication and consistency that makes the entire experience feel seamless.

Over the past two years, we have grown by focusing on the things that matter most to our clients: professionalism, careful attention to detail, and showing up when we say we will. Every visit reflects that commitment.

Whether you need recurring cleaning for your home, a tailored plan for your office, or a turnover for your short term rental, we approach every job with the same standard of exceptional care.
